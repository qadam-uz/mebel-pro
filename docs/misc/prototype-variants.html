UI prototype — 4 design-system variants

The same static prototype (every screen from docs/ref/ux/information-architecture.md) rendered four ways. Click around each, then pick a direction with your team. Same markup & behaviour everywhere — only the design system (tokens, type, spacing, shapes, shadows, the side-nav treatment) differs.

How they differ:  A · Aurora — modern indigo SaaS, balanced, soft shadows (Stripe/Vercel-ish).  B · Carbon — dense power-user console, dark side nav, hairlines, cyan accent (Linear/GitHub-ish).  C · Terra — warm cream surfaces, clay accent, big rounded corners, roomy, friendly type.  D · Mono — high-contrast editorial: black & white, square corners, thick borders, hard offset shadows, heavy uppercase.
