For customers

No more phone calls and verbal prices. Enter your parts, optimise, see everything, order.

📐

Design it yourself

Pick a workshop branch and a material, type in your parts list — dimensions, quantity, grain, edge banding — and run the optimiser.

  • Interactive layout, sheet by sheet
  • Waste %, sheets used, total cut length
  • Edge banding by thickness
  • Iterate freely — each run is saved as a draft
📦

Order & track

Turn a draft into an order — pickup or delivery (fee shown up front), pick how you'll pay, confirm. The price is locked in the moment you order.

  • Live status: confirmed → in production → ready → delivered
  • Modify or cancel while it's still early
  • Sign in with Telegram — no password to remember
  • Mobile‑first — works on the phone in the shop

For workshops

Run the whole shop — branches, catalogue, warehouse, workers, pricing, orders — in one place.

🏬

One system, many branches

  • Branches with materials, stock, workers, pricing per branch
  • An order board: new → confirmed → production → ready → delivery → done
  • Record cash / bank payments; approve pay‑later; process refunds
  • Dashboard: KPIs, status mix, refund SLA, low stock, audit highlights
👥

Owner & staff, done right

  • The owner runs everything; staff get exactly the per‑branch permissions you grant
  • Atomic stock — no double‑spending the last sheet
  • Snapshot pricing — old orders don't drift when you change rates
  • Append‑only audit — who did what, when

How it works

From a parts list to cut panels — a couple of steps.

Pick a branch & material

Choose where you want it cut and which sheet material — from the shop or your own.

Enter parts, optimise

Type or paste your parts; the optimiser lays them out and shows waste, sheets, cut length.

Order it

Pickup or delivery, choose how to pay, confirm — the price and the cutting are locked in.

Track to handover

The workshop cuts to the PDF; you watch it go confirmed → production → ready → delivered.
